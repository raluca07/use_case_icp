# Compute Over the Graph, Do Not Show It

Paper draft for a NeurIPS 2026 workshop, joint with Raluca Crisan (Etiq AI).
Authorship and affiliation order to be confirmed.

## Build

    ./build.sh          # tectonic; outputs build/main.pdf

`neurips_2026.sty` is the official style, used with the `dblblindworkshop` option.
Swap to `[preprint]` in `main.tex` to see the author block while drafting.

## Layout

- `main.tex`, `sections/` — the paper. Body must stay within 9 pages; references
  and appendices are excluded from that limit.
- `refs.bib` — 13 references, every one verified against the arXiv API or CrossRef.
  No entry was written from memory.
- `evidence-map.md` — every quantitative claim traced to the artifact that produced
  it, plus an explicit list of what must not be claimed.
- `paper_plaintext.md` — prose export, matching the shared Google Doc.
- `figures/` — generated by `scripts/make_figures.py` on the benchmark branch.

## Where the numbers come from

The benchmark, corpora and result files live on branch `fabio/issue-3-and-5` under
`src/use_case_icp/` and `benchmark-results/`, and are mirrored in the standalone
artifact repository `graph-trust-bench`.

## Not yet in the PDF

Two completed results are written up in `paper_plaintext.md` and in the commit log
but have not been folded into the LaTeX: the collapsed-helper protocol
(`benchmark-results/collapsed_protocol.json`) and the wrong-sense fault class
(`benchmark-results/wrong_sense.json`). The powered null re-run
(`benchmark-results/powered_null.json`) was still in flight.
